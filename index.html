About Me

Executive Summary

I build things that work — systems, organizations, and governance frameworks that hold up under pressure.

My career has moved across three domains that most people keep separate: enterprise infrastructure engineering, environmental entrepreneurship, and technology risk & governance. That breadth is not a detour. It is the point.

I started by getting my hands deep into enterprise technology, designing and implementing Cisco-based network and infrastructure environments, learning how systems are actually built, where they break, and what it takes to keep them running at scale. That foundation changed how I see risk — not as a checklist, but as a systems problem.

In parallel, I built an award-winning sustainability startup from the ground up, pioneering waste upcycling and circular economy solutions. I ran the technology, operations, compliance, and strategy. Building something real, under constraints, with accountability for outcomes — that is a different education than any classroom offers.

I hold a Master of Engineering Management from Michigan Technological University, and certifications in CISA, CISM, CRISC, and AAISM — including one of the first credentials in AI systems management. I am based in the United States and at my best in environments that value people who think across disciplines and deliver with precision.
